The live FPS HUD counts acquired and submitted drawable frames, not animation callbacks. It updates every half second, keeps numeric paragraphs out of the unbounded text-key cache, and resets on idle. The displayed milliseconds are average frame interval, not GPU timing or an actual display scanout callback. Occluded acquisition attempts do not count. No GPU readback, polling, or separate HUD animation loop is added. The native Metal FPS above is measured before this HUD rebuild.
